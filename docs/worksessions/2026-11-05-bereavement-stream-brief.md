## The institutional ICP

- 42 CFR 418.64(d): every Medicare-certified hospice must provide bereavement services for 13 months after a
  death under a written plan of care. Unreimbursed; run by one Bereavement Coordinator.
- Standard delivery is a mailing schedule (sympathy card, then ~3 wk, 3, 6, 9, 13 months). ~69% build mailings
  in-house, ~30% buy pre-printed (MyNHPCO 2022 survey). Postage up >34% since 2021.
- Universe: 6,706 Medicare-certified hospices US (2024, MedPAC); 184 licensed in Ohio (ODH May 2026);
  15,401 funeral homes (NFDA 2025). Incumbent funeral aftercare card programs bill $20–25/family.
- Named Columbus programs with the role: Capital City Hospice (Addus), OhioHealth at Home / Compassus,
  Ohio's Hospice (OSU Wexner site), OhioHealth Kobacker House. Chains = one approval, many sites.

## Product decision: "Thirteen Months" (working title)

- 6 cards per family (1 sympathy + 5 remembrance), Lacey's existing cardinal/botanical files, artist credit on
  the back, no tattoo call-to-action ever. Lacey approves six images (one hour) and is paid a 12% royalty on
  wholesale. She makes no product.
- Wholesale $15/family set cards-only (no family data touches us, no BAA); $21 with six Feelings Unplugged
  grief-literacy inserts; Measurement Ally add-on $150/program/month (de-identified family survey + annual
  bereavement outcomes report for the plan-of-care evaluation).
- Unit economics (assumptions): print $5.50, ship $1.00, royalty $1.80 → $6.70 gross margin per set (45%).
- Scenarios at 300 families/account: 3 accounts ≈ $13.5k cards + $3.6k measurement; 10 ≈ $45k + $10.8k;
  30 ≈ $135k + $32.4k. Lacey royalty $1.6k / $5.4k / $16.2k.
- Invoice through Measurement Ally as "Raw Sun Art Creatives, a Measurement Ally studio" (woopsi.ink's public
  naming). Hospices pay by PO/check/ACH; no Stripe needed.

## Portfolio slices

RawSunArt supplies the license and earns royalty. RSA Creatives (MA studio) owns product, sales, reorders.
Measurement Ally sells the outcomes service and keeps the de-identified dataset. Feelings Unplugged / USSQ
licenses insert content and can hold a grant-eligible children's-bereavement program line. EliteTatz gets
organic attribution only. woopsi.ink gets cheaper print runs and a "used by hospices" proof line.

## 90-day plan

Weeks 1–2 list (ODH), coordinators for 25 programs, printer quotes, six designs, six inserts, PO order page.
Week 3 print and mail 25 sample packs (~$450). Weeks 4–6 four-touch sequence, 20-min walkthroughs.
Weeks 7–12 close pilots, standing monthly orders, approach Addus/Compassus corporate.

**Decision gate day 90:** ≥3 paying accounts and ≥$5k committed annualized → scale to chains and funeral homes.
Fewer → stop direct sales; license art to the incumbent aftercare vendors.

## Boundaries

Cards-only keeps all family data inside the hospice; survey is de-identified by design. Royalty is a 1099
payment until RawSunArt LLC exists. Never market tattoos to bereaved families or through a hospice.
