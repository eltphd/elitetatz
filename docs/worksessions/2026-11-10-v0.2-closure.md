# RawSunArt × EliteTatz — Work Session Brief · 2026-11-10

**Session focus:** make sure every client message reaches Lacey's email, stop the portal from nagging her or her clients about things already handled, and make the portal card something an artist can follow. Close at a named version.

**Bottom line:** EliteTatz v0.2.0 is live. A client's inquiry now reaches Lacey's inbox once, even if the portal is down, with the client as reply-to so she answers from her own mail app. When she does, the portal hears it, stops the reminders, and the card says "You already answered Ana by email." Every email meant for her leaves a row saying whether it was sent, delivered, skipped or failed, and a failure alerts Erica the same minute. Nine pull requests merged across two repositories, three migrations applied, and one living page shows what the client, the artist and the admin each see.

## Decisions

- **Every client message reaches Lacey's email; the portal is extra.** Erica's Nov 9 rule. The form's email path no longer depends on the portal being up, and the portal emails her before it saves anything, so a database problem cannot lose an inquiry.
- **One email per thing, never two.** The first fix sent each inquiry through the portal and the backup form service at once. Erica reversed it the same day: "i dont want her getting two emails she'll hate that." The backup now fires only when the portal did not get her email out.
- **A failed bot check never turns a real person away.** The inquiry is accepted and tagged "[possible bot]" in her subject line. She decides.
- **The portal never emails her about a message already in her own mailbox.** A forward from her, or a client email that reached her first, opens or updates the lead and sends her nothing.
- **Her email reply counts as her answer.** Emails to her about a client carry two reply addresses: the client, and the portal's intake address. Her reply goes straight to the client and the portal gets a copy. Decided after Erica saw the alternative: reminders about a client she had already answered ("ADDRESS THE GAP THAT GIVES FALSE ANNOYING NUDGES").
- **The portal does not time a conversation it cannot see.** When the last word sits in her mailbox, neither she nor the client gets a waiting reminder. A wrong "still with Lacey" costs more trust than a missed reminder.
- **The intake address is `studio@inbox.rawsunart.com`.** It was `leads@`; clients see it beside their own address on her replies and "leads" reads cold. Every address on that domain still works.
- **Her nudge is at 24 hours; the client's "still with Lacey" note stays at 36.** rawsunart.com promises a reply within 48 hours, so a 24-hour "still waiting" to the client would undercut it.
- **The morning system check mails nobody.** It was first designed to email a test inbox. That became "send nothing", and proof of delivery now comes from the mail provider's own delivery receipts written onto the email log.
- **The assistant is an aid, not the artist.** After Lacey read it as "art ChatGPT comes up with", it was rebuilt as a wording helper: she says what she means, it offers a way to say it for that client, tells her what it changed, asks when unsure. Her yes, no, price and dates are held fixed.
- **The daily-checks release went out after 7:00 PM Eastern** so Lacey's first evening note is tomorrow's, after the first morning check, and does not open with "no system check ran."
- **EliteTatz is not Lacey's page.** Hide her as the only artist now. A public directory returns after at least 10 artists in a region, or once artists can rate each other in public. Measurement Ally sells provenance and leads to artists to fund it. Recorded; not built.
- **This stop is v0.2.0.** Tagged on both repositories.
- **Next: a designer reviews the portal, then separate threads build what they recommend.**

## Open questions & risks

- **Her reply reaching the portal is unproven until tonight.** It depends on her mail app honoring two reply addresses. If the To line shows only the client, note which app she uses.
- **Whether her words appear on the card** depends on her domain's mail authentication passing. If it does not, the card says "Replied by email" and the timers still stop.
- **The wording helper has not run on her real words or the live model from this session.** The tone is hers to judge.
- **No real client has used the form since it went live on Oct 7.** Everything in the log is a test. Traffic, not software, is the constraint on deposits.
- **The inbound path ignores mail from the operator address.** Correct by design, and a trap in testing.
- **Pricing help stops at her own price band.** Using booking and market data to suggest a more profitable number is not built.
- **Text messages and Slack are not connected in production.** Email is the only channel that reaches Lacey or Erica today.

## If these steps are completed

- **Tonight's walkthrough turns a build into a tool Lacey trusts.** One artist using it without help is the proof Erica named as the gate: "if it works for her I can open it up to other artists." The directory threshold in her own plan is 10 artists in a region; the first one has to be real.
- **Each inquiry is worth a $100 deposit and a tattoo priced from $250 an hour**, by the figures in her artist profile. v0.2.0 does not create inquiries; it stops losing them. An inquiry that reaches her inbox once, with the client one reply away, is one she can answer in the minute she reads it.
- **The Reconnect email is where the next deposits come from.** The reviewed list is ready and the send waits on one tap. With v0.2.0 the replies land in her inbox once, the portal learns when she answers, and nobody is told "still with Lacey" after she has written back.
- **The evening note and delivery receipts replace anxiety with a daily fact.** "Today: 0 new inquiries. System checked OK" is the line that lets a quiet day be a quiet day and not a suspected outage.
- **The designer starts from evidence.** Ten observed steps and four answers from the person the product is for, instead of opinions about the screens.

## If these steps stall — the cost of the open loop

- **Unverified, the reply link fails quietly.** If her replies do not reach the portal and nobody checks, the old behavior returns: a client she answered gets "still with Lacey" a day and a half later. That is the exact failure this session was run to remove, and it would arrive after she was told it was fixed.
- **Trust spent is not refunded.** She was confused by the portal once already. A second confusing session, with a helper she has not tried and reminders she does not expect, is how an artist goes back to email only. Then the portal carries a copy of work that happens elsewhere, and there is no first artist to show the second.
- **The platform plan waits on this one relationship.** Hiding her as the only artist, the directory, artists rating artists and the provenance and leads revenue all assume artists who use the tool. None of them start while the first one is unsure.
- **What was built tonight depreciates unvisited.** The walkthrough page, the demo card and the wording helper are most useful while the confusion is fresh for both of them. In two weeks the questions will be answered from memory instead of from the screen.
- **The revenue step is still one tap away and still untaken.** Every week the Reconnect email waits is a week of holiday booking that the kept-client list is not being asked about.
