The test, step by step

  1. Sign in at elitetatz.app with Google or the email link.Then Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Read the walkthrough, tap Got it.Open the example card to see the brief, the transcript and the taps.
  3. Connections → Connect Google Calendar.Google shows “this app isn’t verified” while Erica’s verification is pending. Tap Advanced, then continue. Read-only; it only reads your calendar when you look.
  4. Be a client. On your phone, open rawsunart.com and tap “New idea? Let my concierge shape it with you.” Ask for a small piece, answer its questions, give a personal email that is not lacey@rawsunart.com.You will get the client’s “your inquiry is in” email there, and your artist email at lacey@.
  5. Open the new card. Read the transcript. Tap the assistant for a draft. Tap Ask or Reply and send one line.Check the client address: your line arrived with a link to the inquiry page.
  6. Reply as the client from that inquiry page. Watch it land on the thread.
  7. Quote it, then tap Deposit received, then Confirm date.Each tap changes what the client sees on their page.
  8. Fill the inquiry form on rawsunart.com once too. Same card, no chat.
  9. Pass on both test cards when you are done.
